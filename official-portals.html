How to stay safe online
• Check the domain carefully before entering personal details.
• Never share your OTP or password with someone claiming to be an agent.
• Government fees and procedures can change. Confirm the latest details on the department's official website.
• If a link stops working, use the main Government of Pakistan website to locate the relevant department.
