Stay safe
• Online information ki bunyaad par apni prescribed medicine band ya change na karein.
• Medicine khareedte waqt packaging, expiry aur authorized pharmacy ka khayal rakhein.
• Vaccine eligibility, schedule aur availability relevant health authority se confirm karein.
• Apna CNIC, OTP ya medical records unknown websites par upload na karein.
