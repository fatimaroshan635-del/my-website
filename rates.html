Rate verification checklist
1. Source aur publication date check karein.
2. Currency, unit (jaise per gram ya per tola) aur city confirm karein.
3. Gold aur currency ke liye buying/selling rate ka farq samjhein.
4. Petrol ke liye latest applicable official notification dekhein.
5. Is directory ko investment ya financial advice na samjhein.
