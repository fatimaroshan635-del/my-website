Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us

This Privacy Policy explains how Digital Pakistan handles information when you visit and use our website.

1. About Digital Pakistan

Digital Pakistan provides information and links related to public services, education, jobs, government schemes, Islamic resources, results and other useful online tools. We aim to help visitors find relevant information and official resources.

2. Information We Collect

The information collected may depend on how you use the website and which features or third-party services are enabled. Web hosting, security and analytics services may process technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate location derived from an IP address, requested pages and error logs.

If we introduce a contact form, account feature or another service that asks for personal information, we will explain the relevant information requested and its purpose at that feature.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used, where applicable, to:

  • Operate, maintain and secure the website.
  • Understand technical problems and improve usability.
  • Respond to messages that visitors voluntarily send us.
  • Prevent abuse and comply with applicable legal requirements.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Digital Pakistan or third-party services used on the website may use cookies or similar technologies for functions such as security, preferences, measurement or advertising. Their use depends on which services are active. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; blocking them may affect some website features.

5. Advertising and Google Services

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, its providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalize advertisements, subject to their settings and policies. Advertising providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable consent requirements.

Learn more about Google's advertising practices at Google Ads and Privacy and Google's general Privacy Policy .

6. Third-Party Websites

Our pages may link to government portals, educational websites, public-service resources and other external websites. When you open an external link, that website's own terms and privacy policy apply. We do not control the privacy practices or content of external websites and encourage you to review their policies.

7. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable steps may be taken to protect information handled through the website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Retention periods depend on the type of information, the service involved and applicable requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is intended to provide general information and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through a feature we operate, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

9. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding personal information. You can also manage browser cookies and choose whether to use optional website features. Where relevant, requests can be made using the contact method provided on our website.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features, services or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy, please use the contact details or contact page published on the Digital Pakistan website, if available.
